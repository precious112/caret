# No Black Box: Full Visibility & Control

**Caret operates with complete transparency, showing you every file read and every proposed diff.**

Understand exactly what Caret is doing and why—no obfuscation. Review all actions and approve changes before they're made. Caret uses checkpoints, allowing you to easily revert if needed, maintaining full control over your codebase. With BYO-key, you also have clear cost transparency.
